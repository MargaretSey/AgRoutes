Privacy Policy

Sample text for the prototype

What we collect

Your mobile number, name, farm name, home base, what you grow, and your insurance certificate.

How we use it

To sign you in with a texted code, match you with trips, and let other farmers see that you're verified and insured.

Who sees what

Other farmers see your name, farm, general area and badges. Your street address stays hidden unless you share it in a message. Only AgRoutes reviewers see your insurance certificate.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information or show you ads.

Your choices

Change what's shown in Profile, or delete your account and your data at any time.

Questions

privacy@agroutes.example
